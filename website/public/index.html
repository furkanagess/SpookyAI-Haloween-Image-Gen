FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is SpookyAI free?

The app is free to download and new installs get starter tokens to try it. Each creation uses tokens; you can get more in the app.

What happens to my photos?

Photos are sent securely to our AI provider only to create the result you asked for. Your results are saved on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad from the App Store, and Android phones from Google Play.

Do I need ideas or prompts?

No. Pick a ready-made style or a trend and tap once. You can also type your own idea.
